Privacy Policy — Nyumba Arusha

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Nyumba Arusha collects account information such as email, authentication data, payment transaction records and information necessary to operate the property-contact service. Property contact details are protected and are disclosed only after the service records a successful payment.

Use of data

We use data to authenticate users, operate listings, process/verify payments, prevent fraud, provide customer support and maintain security/audit records.

Sharing

Payment information necessary to process a transaction may be shared with the selected payment provider. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Users can request account deletion from inside the app. Where required for accounting, fraud prevention or legal obligations, some transaction records may be retained for the applicable period.

Security

Passwords are hashed; API access uses authenticated HTTPS in production; payment secrets are kept on the server and not inside the Android app.
